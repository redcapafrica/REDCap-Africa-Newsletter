User Spotlight

Itumeleng Malatji
Wits BITS, University of the Witwatersrand, South Africa

I'm a Biomedical Informatics Officer at the University of the Witwatersrand, and I work as a REDCap administrator. I hold a Bachelor of IT in Business Systems, which shapes how I look at REDCap: not just as a data collection tool, but as a platform that can connect with and extend other systems.

As a data professional, meeting REDCap very early in my career has been amazing. I believe there is always more to do with it, and that belief keeps me curious. What I enjoy most about my role is finding and discovering new things I can do with REDCap.

Recently, I've been exploring external modules and learning how they are built. It has been fascinating to see how the REDCap community extends the platform far beyond its core features, and how much flexibility that gives us as administrators when supporting researchers.

So far, the PDF Injector has been a game changer for me. It lets you fill PDF documents directly from REDCap data, so information captured in a project can flow straight into a formatted document without anyone retyping it. It's a smart solution to a very common problem, and it saves time while reducing errors.

One of my biggest curiosities right now is whether REDCap could act as a password verifier, or a gateway, for other systems. REDCap already handles authentication so well for its own users that I wonder how far it could be extended to serve other applications too. I'm still exploring the idea, and I'd love to hear from other community members who have thought about this.

My advice to other users and administrators is to be curious. Take time to explore the external modules, see what the community has built, and ask whether there's a smarter way to do the things you currently do manually.

I'm proud to be part of the REDCap Africa community and look forward to learning from and sharing with fellow users.
