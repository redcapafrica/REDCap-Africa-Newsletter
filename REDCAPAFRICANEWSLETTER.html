REDCap Africa at REDCapCon 2026

REDCap Africa was proudly represented at REDCapCon 2026, joining colleagues from around the world to share experiences, strengthen collaborations, and explore the latest innovations in research data management.

A highlight of the conference was the presentation of the poster, Advancing Research In Africa, which showcased our ongoing efforts to advance REDCap implementation, support high-quality research, and build data management capacity across Africa.
