REDCap Africa Symposium 2026 at a Glance

The REDCap Africa Symposium 2026 brought together 89 delegates from 9 countries, creating a vibrant space for learning, collaboration, and knowledge exchange across the continent. Throughout the week, participants engaged in discussions on research data management, emerging innovations, and shared experiences from their respective institutions. The symposium fostered meaningful connections among researchers, data professionals, and REDCap users, reinforcing a shared commitment to advancing research excellence across Africa.

The symposium also featured two dedicated training opportunities: the Advanced Workshop and the System Administrator Training Workshop, providing attendees with hands-on learning, practical skills development, and opportunities to deepen their expertise in REDCap administration and advanced project management.

A special highlight of the week was the 10-Year Celebratory Dinner, where delegates, speakers, and partners came together to celebrate a decade of growth, collaboration, and impact within the REDCap Africa community. The evening provided an opportunity to reflect on key milestones and achievements over the past ten years, while looking ahead to the continued advancement of research data management and innovation across the continent.

As we conclude another successful REDCap Africa Symposium, we extend our sincere thanks to everyone who joined us, contributed their expertise, and helped make this event such a memorable experience. See you in the city of gold!

User Spotlight

Lingstone Chiume
Institution,Country

For over 12 years, I have been working in clinical data management. I’ve always looked at REDCap as more than just a place to store data. To me, it is a core piece of research infrastructure that can be built upon to support our larger goals. Over the years, I have used REDCap to manage and run several complex, longitudinal studies. My work involves building workflows that make life easier for researchers out in the field. For instance, managing offline data dictionaries has been essential for our teams who need to collect data in areas with unreliable internet.
