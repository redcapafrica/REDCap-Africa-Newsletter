User Spotlight

Manlafi Keita
ALERRT Consortium, MRC Unit The Gambia at LSHTM

I currently work within a pool of data managers at MRC Unit the Gambia @ LSHTM, where we support research projects both within the Unit and across the sub-region. I serve as the focal Data Manager for the ALERRT consortium under the supervision of our Head of Department, Bai Lamin Dondeh. Within the ALERRT consortium, I contribute to Work Package 3 (WP3), which focuses on developing robust data systems, ensuring data quality and standardization, and supporting secure, multi-country data collection and sharing. Over the years, we have leveraged Redcap and its functionalities to support a range of projects, including the FISSA Study, Long COVID, CCP, the ACE2 Polymorphism Study, and the ongoing SeroMARV study.
