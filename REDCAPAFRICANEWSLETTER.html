Welcome to this edition of the REDCap Africa Newsletter. Following an inspiring and impactful week at the REDCap Africa Symposium 2026, we are still energized by the conversations, connections, and ideas shared across our growing community. From thought-provoking keynotes and practical workshops to meaningful networking and collaboration, the symposium highlighted the power of working together to strengthen research data management across Africa. In this edition, we reflect on some of the key moments, celebrate our community, and share what's next as we continue advancing research excellence across the continent.

REDCap Africa Symposium 2026 at a Glance

The REDCap Africa Symposium 2026 brought together 89 delegates from 9 countries, creating a vibrant space for learning, collaboration, and knowledge exchange across the continent. Throughout the week, participants engaged in discussions on research data management, emerging innovations, and shared experiences from their respective institutions. The symposium fostered meaningful connections among researchers, data professionals, and REDCap users, reinforcing a shared commitment to advancing research excellence across Africa.

The symposium also featured two dedicated training workshops, providing attendees with hands-on learning, practical skills development, and opportunities to deepen their expertise in REDCap administration and advanced project management.

A special highlight of the week was the 10-Year Celebration Event, where delegates came together to celebrate a decade of growth, collaboration, and impact within the REDCap Africa community. The informal gathering provided an opportunity for attendees to connect, reflect on the journey of REDCap Africa over the past ten years, and celebrate the community's achievements while looking ahead to future opportunities for collaboration and innovation across the continent. The event was generously sponsored by DIPLOMICS

As we conclude another successful REDCap Africa Symposium, we extend our sincere thanks to everyone who joined us, contributed their expertise, and helped make this event such a memorable experience. We are grateful to our host, the University of Cape Town (UCT), and our collaborators, the South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC) and DIPLOMICS, whose support contributed greatly to the success of the symposium. See you next year in the City of Gold!

User Spotlight

Lingstone Chiume
Johns Hopkins Project, Malawi

For over 12 years, I have been working in clinical data management. I've always looked at REDCap as more than just a place to store data. To me, it is a core piece of research infrastructure that can be built upon to support our larger goals.

Over the years, I have used REDCap to manage and run several complex, longitudinal studies. My work involves building workflows that make life easier for researchers out in the field. For instance, managing offline data dictionaries has been essential for our teams who need to collect data in areas with unreliable internet.
