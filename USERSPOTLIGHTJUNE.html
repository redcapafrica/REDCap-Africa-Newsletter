User Spotlight

Edwin Mkwanazi
Africa Health Research Institute (AHRI), South Africa

I am a Senior Data Scientist based in Durban, South Africa, with over 20 years of experience in data management. I hold a BSc in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics and I am currently pursuing a PGDip in eHealth at the University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN). My career has focused on designing, building, and supporting data management systems across both clinical and non-clinical research environments, as well as contributing to system strengthening initiatives in the public health sector. I started using REDCap in 2014, and since then it has become a central tool in my work. I use it to design and manage data collection processes across a wide range of projects, including building electronic data capture instruments, managing longitudinal datasets, and supporting end-to-end data workflows from collection to analysis.

My work with REDCap spans epidemiological research, clinical and non-clinical trials, and program monitoring and evaluation. I have previously been involved in projects such as HOPE (Wits RHI), AIR-SA (AHRI), and PrEP-SMART (Wits RHI). The HOPE study focuses on evaluating the effectiveness of one-dose and two-dose HPV vaccination strategies in reducing HPV prevalence among adolescent girls. The AIR-SA study investigates improved treatment strategies for asthma in children and adolescents using anti-inflammatory reliever therapies. The PrEP-SMART study evaluates innovative approaches to improving adherence to HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) among young women.

I am currently supporting clinical trials including ATTIS, M72, and BNT164, where robust data systems are essential for ensuring data quality, regulatory compliance, and efficient trial operations. The ATTIS study (Asymptomatic TB Transmission in Indonesia and South Africa) is investigating whether individuals with asymptomatic tuberculosis can transmit the disease, helping to improve understanding of TB transmission dynamics. The M72 trial is a Phase 3 study evaluating the safety and effectiveness of a novel tuberculosis vaccine candidate aimed at preventing active TB disease. The BNT164 trial is an early-phase study assessing the safety and immune response of an investigational mRNA-based tuberculosis vaccine.

I am particularly passionate about systems integration and automation, as well as synchronising data flows across platforms. Taking advantage of REDCap’s API and scripting capabilities, I have enjoyed transforming and automating several key operational processes. These include: participant reimbursement systems at AHRI, where I integrated REDCap with PayCentral to streamline and automate payment workflows, participant follow-up processes, where I integrated TeleRivet with REDCap to enhance communication, tracking, and participant retention, enumeration and field data collection workflows, where I integrated Leafy Maps with REDCap to enable geospatial data capture and improve field operations These integrations have significantly improved efficiency, reduced manual workload, and strengthened the accuracy and timeliness of data across projects.

One of the most impactful aspects of REDCap is its ability to balance flexibility with strong data governance. It enables rapid deployment of data collection tools while maintaining audit trails and ensuring data integrity an essential requirement for both research and public health programs. A key lesson I have learned is the importance of investing time in the design phase of any REDCap project. Carefully structured data collection instruments significantly reduce the need for downstream data cleaning and improve the overall efficiency and reliability of the project. Overall, REDCap has been instrumental in supporting my work in building resilient, integrated data systems and advancing data-driven decision-making across a range of health and research initiatives.
