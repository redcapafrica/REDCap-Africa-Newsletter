User Spotlight

Lwazi Mhlanti
Africa Health Research Institute (AHRI), South Africa

Born in Qumbu, Transkei, Lwazi developed an early interest in technology. One of his hobbies was repairing electronics for friends and family. His experience working in his mother's internet café further fueled his passion for computers, turning his hobby into a valuable skill. Recognizing his talent, he pursued additional IT training to enhance his expertise.

I am the REDCap administrator at the CRC at the University of Cape Town (UCT), which is the commercial arm of the university dedicated to supporting industry collaborations and applied research. We support the entire research side of the university with any REDCap queries. My self and my manager (Annemie Stewart) are the last line of support for REDCap users, and we also collaborate with ICTS by testing and coordinating software updates and new feature implementations to ensure REDCap remains current and secure. We are always open to new collaborations with external partners interested in leveraging REDCap for their research needs.

I use REDCap to develop and maintain large databases for clinical trials and healthcare studies across Africa. Many of these projects are randomized controlled trials that utilize REDCap’s built-in Randomization module to assign participants to different study arms securely and efficiently online, facilitating multi-site trial management across the continent. To enhance data security and site-specific access, Data Access Groups (DAGs) are used to ensure that different sites can view only their own data. The platform manages complex datasets and ensures data quality through validation and audit trails, complemented by the use of the data resolution workflow to resolve data discrepancies and ensure high-quality data.

We also have an automated notification system that alerts the study team when a patient is enrolled, ensuring timely follow-up and data entry. Additionally, because we are collecting a large volume of data, embedded fields within REDCap forms are very useful—they help streamline data entry by allowing users to input data directly into specific fields without navigating away from the form, reducing errors and improving efficiency.

I also find REDCap’s flexibility and user-friendly interface very helpful for creating customized forms and automating tasks like participant invitations. A useful tip is to leverage automation features for follow-ups and regularly review user permissions to ensure data security. Overall, REDCap is an essential tool that enables efficient, secure, and scalable data management for diverse research projects at UCT.
